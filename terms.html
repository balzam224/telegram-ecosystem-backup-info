Last updated: 17 November 2026.

This utility is being configured for private use by the owner of the application server and Google Drive account. It is not a public storage service and does not offer subscriptions or paid backup services.

The owner is responsible for authorizing access to their Google account, maintaining adequate storage, protecting credentials and keeping the archive encryption key available for recovery.

Backup checks reduce the chance of unusable archives but do not guarantee continuous availability or recovery of every external action. A restoration must be reviewed before application services resume, including any pending message delivery.

The owner may stop the utility and revoke Google authorization at any time. Google services remain subject to Google's own terms and storage policies.
